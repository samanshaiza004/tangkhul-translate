When you submit a correction, we store the corrected English translation you provide. We also store any note you write and any issue tags you select, such as a tag saying that information is missing or the English sounds unnatural. These details stay connected to the translation you are responding to.

We keep your contribution so it can be reviewed and, if appropriate, used to improve the translator. A human will review every contribution before it is used for anything. Your correction, note, and tags are never accepted or used automatically.

Please do not include private or sensitive information in the correction or note. This includes medical, legal, or financial information, as well as details that could clearly identify you or another person. Avoid names, addresses, account numbers, identification numbers, contact details, or other highly identifying information unless they are ordinary parts of the source text and are safe to share.

By submitting the form, you confirm that you are okay with your contribution being stored and reviewed as described above.
